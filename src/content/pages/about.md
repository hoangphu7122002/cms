I am Nguyễn Đắc Hoàng Phú (Hoang Phu). This blog is where I share what I learn while working and studying: what I tried, what the numbers said, and what went wrong on the way.

The posts today come from one project, a serving system for large language models on GPUs. I measure engines, memory and cost, and write down the results with the limits of each measurement. Later posts may cover other things I learn.
